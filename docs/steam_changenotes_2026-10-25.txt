[h1]25 October 2026[/h1]

[h2]New numpad keys[/h2]
Changed keys before? Check ESC > Options > Controls > Configure Addons > 1st JMSB.
[list]
[*][b]1-6[/b] pick net 1-6. [b]Ctrl + 1-6[/b] mute or unmute it.
[*][b]Alt + 1-4[/b] device keys F1-F4.
[*][b]0 / .[/b] previous / next net. On the tablet they scroll the open screen first.
[*][b]7[/b] voice activation on/off. [b]8[/b] tablet. [b]9[/b] Troops In Contact.
[*][b]*[/b] answer / hang up. [b]/[/b] talk local (hold). [b]- / +[/b] voice volume.
[*][b]Enter[/b] is not used. Talk on your net with Push To Talk.
[*]Arma's [b]Push To Talk (Direct)[/b] also talks local. Set it in Options > Controls > Multiplayer.
[/list]

[h2]Artillery[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Counter-battery is back.[/b] The enemy fires back at player-crewed guns. TAC//SUPPORT virtual artillery is exempt. Values on the Enemy Reaction module.
[*][b]CB radar.[/b] Every enemy shell sends your artillery team a TAC//MSG from CB RADAR, pinned on the map. Set the team in the PAC ORBAT: squad kind art or mortar.
[*][b]ARTY app and tile.[/b] Fire solutions from ACE's own tables. Target by typed grid, map marker or radar fix. MRSI up to 3 rounds.
[*][b]Artillery carriers.[/b] M-ATV GMG and HMG, DAGOR armed and AT, AMV-7 Marshall. All four US camos. Each as Mortar Carrier and Rocket Artillery.
[*]Driver's ACE menu: Pre-deploy puts a baseplate 2.5 m behind the vehicle. Deploy sets up the gun in 60 s. Pack takes it down.
[*]Rocket Artillery needs Western Sahara, else it uses the mortar. The baseplate needs ACE CSW.
[/list]
