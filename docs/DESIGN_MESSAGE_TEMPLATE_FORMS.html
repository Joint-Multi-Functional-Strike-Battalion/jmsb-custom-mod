TAC//MSG templates side by side

Each tasking template drawn twice: the standard form on the left, the short form on the right. Marked lines in the standard form are the ones the short form keeps. * = required: the template will not send without it. Layout only: this is not the TAC//MSG skin. Line detail and the comparison with today's templates are in the templates doc; the flows are in the message trees.
