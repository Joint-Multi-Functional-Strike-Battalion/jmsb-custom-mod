[h1]1st Joint Multi-Functional Strike Battalion - Custom Mod[/h1]

The custom mod of the [b]1st Joint Multi-Functional Strike Battalion (1st JMSB)[/b], a near-future (2040) milsim unit. Built for our operations, public for everyone.

[b]Unit site:[/b] [url=https://jmsb.info/]jmsb.info[/url]

It is modular: parts that build on another mod switch off quietly when that mod is not loaded.

[b]Requires[/b] CBA_A3, ACE and DUI - Squad Radar.

[b]Note:[/b] it ships the unit's server-forced CBA settings - review them before running it on your own server.

[img]https://jmsb.info/steam/header_features.png[/img]

[h2]Factions[/h2]
[list]
[*]1st JMSB, US Army JTF (Woodland, Desert, OCP, Tropical) and Marines (Woodland, Desert)
[*]Russia (with Arctic and Arid), China (with Desert), Iran (with Tropical), Turkey, HIMF, EUDF (with Arctic and Desert), ADF, Delhi Accord and Gendarmerie
[/list]

[h2]Future Ammunition (2040)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tiered near-future ammunition for small arms, launchers, vehicle cannons and missiles, across the base game and supported mods
[/list]

[h2]Night vision and AiTR[/h2]
[list]
[*]Full-screen NVGs with night vision and thermal built in
[*]ECOTI / AiTR - aided target recognition: line-of-sight boxes coloured by side, with labels (PERSON | ARMED | 240 m)
[*]Friendly players on the net show a card: name, role, squad, health, radio channel and ACE report
[*]Day and NVG mode, off per player, hidden while the map is open
[*]Goggles see through every scope, including CBA picture-in-picture scopes
[/list]

[h2]TAC//PAD and TAC//PAC[/h2]
[list]
[*]TAC//PAD tablet: live status tiles, squad table, messaging, radio tuning, support and report forms
[*]Blue Force Tracking (ported from TCA)
[*]TAC//PAC: roles, ORBAT, arsenals and radio plan from the unit's database
[/list]

[h2]Systems[/h2]
[list]
[*]ACRE2 radio mesh relaying, jamming and electronic warfare, SatCom
[*]Hacking, drones and UAS, close air support, QRF, logistics, repair, towing and boarding
[*]Medical: Advanced Combat Medicine integration, med bags, CASEVAC
[*]ALiVE adapter, Zeus modules, safe start, spectator, respawn
[*]TAC//ADMIN admin panel
[/list]

[h2]Gear[/h2]
[list]
[*]Uniforms, vests, helmets and backpacks for every faction
[*]Optics, including XM157 fire-control versions of SPS Equipment's Vortex Spitfire
[*]ACE Arsenal Extended configs for attachments, ammunition, NVGs and gear
[/list]
